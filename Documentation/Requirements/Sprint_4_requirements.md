# Sprint 4

## The Solo Sprint

The expectation is that you will design and implement all of this on your own.  You will not be working with your squads outside of moral support.

**Part 1: 50% Demonstrate networked capability \- May 6th**

We have been testing our sprints through automated means or running them all on the same computer.  These practices hide the amazing fact that we can run these programs on separate computers.  For this task you will run Sprint 1,2,3 on different computers during class.  Your demonstration will show that all 3 applications will work, together, on different systems.  

Your code should already be loaded and tested on the 3 machines before class and only requires that you login and run the programs.  You will have a short time to present your working system before you log off and let the next student log in to test their system.  Be prepared before class\!

You may choose to demonstrate your code by hand, or you can develop automated testing for the demonstration.  Your choice.  

**Part 2: 50%** **Choose your own Adventure** \- **April 29, May 11**

Propose, design, implement, test, and present an addition to your Semester long project code.  

* Your proposal must be useful.  It is your responsibility to explain its usefulness.  
* Your design must be future proof. Additions to your idea can be extended upon later.    
  * If there is no way to extend your idea, then it is not a good fit for this sprint.  
* Your design must use a design pattern (observer pattern is off limits), however all other patterns from the book are OK as well as any patterns we have not studied in class.  
* All implementations must be tested.  
* You will submit electronic as well as printed documents for your final sprint.

**April 29th** \- Design Documents are due in digital and paper form.  
**May 11, 5AM**\- 5 minute Video Presentation of your working Sprint 4, in addition to your repository and self assessment.

**You will have an opportunity to make up any remaining technical debt on May 16th**
